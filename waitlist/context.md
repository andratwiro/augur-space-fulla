# Waitlist — project brief (internal)

This note never publishes. `garden/context.md` is the single source of truth for the
garden itself (people, plots, harvest, rota); this file only adds the waiting list.

## The problem

Nine people are waiting for a plot. Until one opens they hear nothing at all, which
reads as being forgotten. Three of nine gave up before their turn came, and none of
them had ever been told where they stood.

This came out of the volunteer retention research, where it is the experiment sticky
"Queue postcards each season. You are 4th in line." The prototype is that experiment
made real, so the research board and the prototype tell one story.
