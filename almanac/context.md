# Almanac — project brief (internal)

This note never publishes. `garden/context.md` is the single source of truth for the
garden itself (people, plots, harvest, rota); this file only covers the almanac.

## The problem

New members sow from the back of the seed packet, and the seed packet is written for
somewhere else. The riera runs dry in summer and holds cold in the low corner through
spring, so the packet dates are wrong here by a few weeks in both directions. The
knowledge lives with whoever has gardened this ground longest, and it leaves when
they do.

## The almanac

A year of Hort de la Riera as one wheel, so a whole season is legible at a glance
rather than as twelve separate lists. Tap a crop band to read its notes. The month
you are in gets the spotlight.

- **Fava beans:** sow October to November, direct in the ground once the autumn rains
  settle. Saturday is the communal sowing day at The New Bed.
- Late sowings feed the winter salads, and shade cloth helps the seedlings start.
- The wheel follows the riera microclimate rather than the seed packets. Assumpta is
  the one who actually knows, so she is who it quotes.
